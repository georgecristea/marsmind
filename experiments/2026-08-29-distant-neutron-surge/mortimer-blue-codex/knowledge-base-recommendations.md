# Knowledge-base recommendations

These are proposals only. No strategy or harness file was changed.

## 1. Deployment schedule must dominate theoretical discount value

When Business Contacts offered Earth Catapult and Asteroid Mining, the raw evaluator favored Asteroid Mining. Earth Catapult was selected for future discounts but never played. Add a rule for late or crowded hands: a discount card must name at least two already-affordable, scheduled follow-ups before it beats an independently productive card.

Evidence conditions: two-player Corporate Era, generation 5 acquisition, Research Outpost already active, Earth Catapult remained unplayed through game end.

## 2. Two-player Ecoline should price plant-to-city geometry more highly

Ecoline's longer runway and seven-plant conversion repeatedly turned placement bonuses into immediate greeneries. The best line paired a central city cluster with Noctis City, Protected Valley, and plant-bonus hexes. Add a two-player adjustment: city-adjacent plant bonuses are both tempo and endgame VP, not merely resource value.

## 3. Award sweep can be punished by milestones plus clock control

Maxwell spent 42 MC funding all three awards and earned 15 VP. Mortimer spent 16 MC on two milestones and earned 10 VP, then redirected the 26 MC difference into parameters and tiles. Add a principle: when an opponent pre-funds multiple awards, do not contest weak metrics; claim efficient milestones and shorten the game so the opportunity cost remains real.

## 4. Repeatable parameter engines gain value when paired with a feeder already in hand

GHG Producing Bacteria alone is a two-generation temperature cadence. Extreme-Cold Fungus turned it into one temperature step per generation and remained actionable after its play requirement expired. The KB already calls the combination elite; add the operational note that the max-temperature requirement only gates playing Extreme-Cold Fungus, not using its action later.

## 5. Endgame research must reprice already-maxed globals to zero

Imported Hydrogen was machine-tagged KEEP after the ocean track was maxed because its nominal ocean was still valued. Add a hard evaluator rule: a global step or ocean with no remaining track space is worth zero unless the card has separately valuable effects.

## 6. In two-player games, re-estimate the clock aggressively after a parameter burst

The game looked long through the early engine phase, then moved from −22°C/5%/1 ocean to completion over generations 9–12. Add a reforecast trigger when one player can produce three or more global steps in a generation; this should shift buys from production to direct parameters and VP immediately.
