# Strategy analysis

## Executive verdict

Mortimer won by converting an initially fragile Ecoline economy into an overwhelming board-and-TR lead, then ending the game before Mining Guild's superior production could convert into enough points. The decisive comparison was not production: it was **58–27 TR, 11–3 greeneries, and two milestones to one**.

## Opening: commit to tempo without starving the engine

The initial Ecoline choice paired directly with Towing A Comet and Noctis City. Mohole Area on the two-plant ocean space established four heat production and accelerated the first Ecoline greenery. Towing A Comet plus a same-turn plant conversion then produced three TR, an ocean, and a greenery in generation 2.

Maxwell opened Mining Guild into Corporate Stronghold, Earth Office, and early metal production. Hackers was his strongest disruption: Mortimer fell to −2 MC production. Energy Tapping stole the exposed energy production, and Noctis City converted that energy into +3 MC production, repairing Mortimer to +1 while establishing the central city/greenery cluster.

## Middle game: milestones and board geometry

Research Outpost was the bridge from tempo to card efficiency. Its isolated city also advanced Mayor and discounted the subsequent pipeline. Business Contacts found Earth Catapult and Noctis Farming; Earth Catapult never deployed, but Noctis Farming later produced an immediate VP, an Ecological Zone trigger, plants, and MC production.

Mortimer claimed Gardener in generation 5. When Maxwell reached Builder and claimed it, Mortimer used first-player generation 8 to play Immigrant City and claim Mayor in the same turn, securing the final milestone slot. Protected Valley and repeated Ecoline conversions expanded the city-scoring forest while Maxwell spent 42 MC funding Banker, Scientist, and Miner.

## Closing engine: parameters, not production

GHG Producing Bacteria and Extreme-Cold Fungus became the temperature engine. Lava Flows, Convoy From Europa, Comet, Investment Loan, standard projects, and Giant Ice Asteroid then compressed the clock. Insects repaired the Small Animals production attack and restored plant production from 1 to 4.

The final sequence was especially efficient:

1. Fungus fed GHG for the 0°C step and its free ocean.
2. Giant Ice Asteroid maxed oceans and removed Maxwell's plants.
3. Heat reached 6°C.
4. A standard asteroid reached 8°C after Maxwell maxed oxygen.
5. Final production yielded 10 plants, enabling the last Ecoline greenery.

## What decided the 26-point margin

- Mortimer: 58 TR, 21 tile VP, 10 milestone VP, 3 card VP.
- Maxwell: 27 TR, 6 tile VP, 5 milestone VP, 15 award VP, 13 card VP.
- Mortimer's two milestones neutralized much of Maxwell's award sweep at far lower total cost.
- Maxwell's production engine was real—15 MC, 3 steel, 2 titanium, and 4 plant production—but the game ended before another full payoff cycle.
- Plant-removal attacks were answered by converting immediately whenever Ecoline reached seven plants.

## In-hindsight alternative

Business Contacts offered Asteroid Mining alongside Earth Catapult. The calculator favored Asteroid Mining; the advisor chose Earth Catapult for theoretical discounts, but Earth Catapult remained unplayed. That was the clearest avoidable selection error. It did not change the result, but the deployed-card schedule should have outweighed speculative discount value.
