# The Blue Forests of Distant Neutron Surge 🌌

Mars began under two competing promises. Maxwell's Mining Guild arrived in red, drilling into the crust and raising steel towers wherever the ground glittered. Mortimer's Blue Ecoline came more quietly—with seeds, reservoirs, and a stubborn belief that the planet could be made alive. 🌱

The Guild struck first. A fortified stronghold rose, furnaces roared, and hackers tore holes in Ecoline's finances. Mortimer answered with an audacious energy raid, then lit Noctis City against the dark. Around it, blue forests began to spread.

Maxwell built an industrial empire: geothermal plants, orbital docks, alloy works, observatories, and a space elevator. History promised to remember him as Mars's great banker, scientist, and miner. 🚀🏗️ Yet every new factory bought Ecoline another season to root itself deeper.

Then the planet changed quickly. Comets cracked the sky. Oceans flooded ancient basins. Lava poured across Arsia Mons. Mortimer's researchers linked cold-loving fungi to greenhouse microbes, turning microscopic life into a planetary furnace. 🌊☄️

The rivalry grew sharp. Maxwell's agents damaged Ecoline's plant nurseries; Mortimer's falling asteroids stripped Maxwell's stored vegetation before it could become forest. Still, the blue canopy expanded—around Noctis, around the immigrant settlements, across protected valleys, and finally beside the lonely Research Outpost.

In the last era, Ecoline seized the heavens. A giant ice asteroid delivered two seas at once. Heat engines drove the atmosphere upward. One final impact completed the warming, and Mars crossed the threshold into a living world. 🔥🌍

When the chronicles closed, Maxwell's red empire remained rich, brilliant, and metallic. But Mortimer's blue legacy was everywhere: in the breathable air, in eleven great forests, in shining cities bordered by green, and in the oceans themselves. Ecoline had not merely prospered on Mars—it had remade Mars in its own image. 🏆

## Mortimer

You will be remembered as the gardener who weaponized patience. Every attack became another reason to grow, and the final blue forest sealed a sweeping triumph. 🌱💙

## Maxwell

You built the planet's industrial spine and claimed its great institutions. Red steel reached the sky—even if, in the end, the forests grew around it. 🔴🚀
