# Technical insights

## Mortimer — Blue Ecoline — 92 VP

### Engine and score

- Final score: 58 TR, 10 milestone VP, 11 greenery VP, 10 city-adjacency VP, 3 card VP.
- Final public board: 3 cities, 11 greeneries, 3 special tiles.
- Key production arc: 2 plant production at start; Mohole Area to 4 heat; Hackers pushed MC production to −2; Noctis City repaired it; Insects restored plant production after Small Animals; final production was 2 MC, 4 plants, 6 heat.
- Ecological Zone finished on 6 animals for 3 VP.

### Best moves

1. Mohole Area on a plant bonus into Towing A Comet plus immediate Ecoline conversion.
2. Energy Tapping into Noctis City after Hackers, turning an attack into the city anchor.
3. Gardener, then first-player Immigrant City plus Mayor to secure two milestones.
4. Extreme-Cold Fungus plus GHG Producing Bacteria, followed by the generations 9–12 parameter rush.
5. Giant Ice Asteroid plus the final asteroid standard project to end the game before Mining Guild's production paid again.

### Best unplayed line

Asteroid Mining over Earth Catapult from Business Contacts was the clearest alternative. Earth Catapult remained in hand; Asteroid Mining would at least have deployed an independent production-and-VP asset. In the actual line, however, direct parameters still dominated both.

### Improvement

Apply the “scheduled follow-ups” test to discount cards. If the discount itself cannot be played in the next generation without displacing a stronger line, take the independently valuable card.

## Maxwell — Red Mining Guild — 66 VP

### Engine and score

- Final score: 27 TR, 5 milestone VP, 15 award VP, 3 greenery VP, 3 city VP, 13 card VP.
- Final public board: 1 city, 3 greeneries.
- Final production: 15 MC, 3 steel, 2 titanium, 4 plants, 1 energy.
- Small Animals reached 4 animals. Search For Life's final resource status is represented in the authoritative app score.

### Best moves

1. Corporate Stronghold plus Mining Guild metal bonuses created an early steel engine.
2. Hackers forced Mortimer to repair a −2 MC-production position.
3. Space Elevator and Advanced Alloys made metal resources highly liquid.
4. Funding Banker, Scientist, and Miner secured all 15 available award VP.
5. Ice Asteroid and repeated greeneries helped prevent an even earlier ending.

### Best public alternative

The information firewall prevents reconstructing Maxwell's hidden unplayed cards. From public state, the strongest alternative was earlier parameter pressure instead of funding all three awards and continuing production. That would have reduced Mortimer's long Ecoline runway and protected the value of Maxwell's 15 award VP.

### Improvement

Once Mortimer held a large TR/tile lead, switch from production to endgame conversion sooner. The final production engine was impressive but still stranded substantial value when the game ended.
