# Strategy KB 6 — Lessons from observed play (ledger)
Format: `- RULE (imperative) · price: VP/TR/MC observed · (EVIDENCE · ×N class)`.
The evidence keeps only what generalises: player count, expansions (e.g. WGT),
generation(s), seat role ("the player" = the advised seat, "a rival", "the
winner", "all seats"). No names, no dates, no game numbers. `×N` = times
independently observed: ×1 is PROVISIONAL (weigh against the 01–05 prices),
×2+ is confirmed. Class: `seats` (harness seat agents) or `humans` (real
opponents) — discount seats-class timing lessons at a human table.
FOLD DISCIPLINE (every post-game fold, in this order): (1) AMEND a
conflicting 01–05 prior in place; (2) INCREMENT an existing rule's ×N when
re-observed (append its new price only if it sharpens the old); (3) only then
APPEND a genuinely new rule. Near-duplicate appends are format violations —
the guard tests enforce tags, dedup and section ceilings.
OUTCOME LOOP: at each postmortem, every 06 rule cited in that game's plans is
judged helped / hurt / wash → increment ×N, amend, or demote to a caveat.
Measured prices here override the priors in 01–05.

## Milestones / awards
- Claim the turn you qualify — the claim goes BEFORE engine cards, and it is a TURN-ORDER rule: as first player, claim before a rival who qualifies later in the same round · price: −10 VP swing when the 8th tag sat in hand and both actions went to cards; +10 VP for 16 MC with both claims one turn from lost; a Gardener claimed while a rival sat on 12 plants — her third greenery landed three log lines later for nothing · (4p WGT gens 7-8, 3p gen 10, both sides · ×4 seats)
- Fund an award only if you can LOCK the metric by the last generation — price every rival's reachable max including last-gen production cards and their unspent bank, prefer the metric your position OWNS structurally over one you merely lead mid-game, and when the gate reads NOT LOCKABLE price the decision against the counterfactual where NOBODY scores the award, never against your own second place: funding opens a pot the RIVAL can win, and "2nd still pays 2" is the wrong number · price: Banker funded gen 2 lost 22-18 to a gen-10 Zeppelins (8 MC → 2 VP not 5); a structurally-owned MC-prod award funded 12v5v4 finished 25v8; 8 MC spent against a verbatim "7 tiles vs a rival's reachable 9 — not lockable" verdict at the cash-the-bucket point paid the winner 5 VP and the player 2, where unfunded the same board reads 67-65 the player's way instead of 70-69 against — the entire 1-point margin · (4p WGT gens 2/8/10, 3p gen 6 · ×4 seats)
- Protect a funded award with one more metric card when a rival is within reach; 43 MC unspent in gen 10 buys the lock · price: −3 VP · (4p WGT gen 10, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Fund the award you lead decisively instead of a 1-VP card, and do not cash the resource that holds the lead if cashing also ends the game while you are behind — the discriminator is the gate's VERDICT WORD, not the generation number: a final-generation funding on LOCKABLE is correct at any price the bank covers, the same gate that refuses an earlier one on NOT LOCKABLE · price: 12-13 heat led Thermalist all game, unfunded; 20 MC → +5 VP for −1 TR vs 21 MC → 1 VP; Thermalist funded in the FINAL generation for 20 MC on "10 heat vs a rival's reachable 9 — LOCKABLE", the heat then deliberately held un-converted → 5 VP · (4p WGT gens 9-10, a rival and the player · ×2 seats)
- From gen 8 count award 2nd-place ties — one tag can be 2 VP · price: a science tag broke a 3-way Scientist tie = the 2-VP margin · (4p WGT gen 8, the player · ×1 seats)
- Fund an uncontested award as soon as the metric is settled · price: Scientist 14 MC → 5 VP · (4p WGT gen 8, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Two awards funded in one turn are the cheapest VP on the board once both metrics are locked · price: Landlord 8 + Banker 14 → 10 VP (2.2 MC/VP) vs SP greenery ~7 MC/VP · (4p gen 8, the player · ×1 seats)
- An award a rival funded is a free target when its metric is a stock you can bank: cut their production of it and stop converting yours · price: Heat Trappers −2 heat prod + one refused conversion = Thermalist 1st +5 for 9 MC · (3p gens 9-14, the player · ×1 seats)
- Read an award's scoring from the engine, never infer it from what a tile is — LANDLORD EXCLUDES OCEAN TILES, so a repeatable ocean engine does nothing for it · price: two generations of plans priced an ocean action as a Landlord lever · (3p gens 4-6, the player · ×1 seats)
- Pick the milestone whose metric your CASH controls, not the one your draws control · price: a card-gated Builder hung tied 6-6 for four generations; money-gated Mayor never wavered · (3p gens 3-8, the player · ×1 seats)
- Bound a rival's milestone distance by their cash plus MULTI-STEP cards, not one increment per turn: a space/event seat with money can jump 2-3 TR in one play · price: a rival's Giant Ice Asteroid took Terraformer between the player's turns while a two-turn route was still in progress — 5 VP lost to a one-step bound · (3p gens 10-11, a rival · ×1 seats)
- One card can carry three milestone metrics at once (science/city/building = tag + tile + tag); pay for it in idle metal · price: 18 MC paid as 8 steel + 2 MC and Builder completed the same turn · (3p gen 7, the player · ×1 seats)
- Sell dead cards to fund a claim you cannot otherwise afford — 1 MC each beats 3 VP/card of nothing · price: four expired cards sold for 4 MC bought a 5 VP milestone that also denied the last slot · (3p gen 8, the player · ×1 seats)

## Tiles & cities
- Build the tile plan the same turn as the city: a plant source (Bushes-class or 2 plant prod) by gen 5; city + 2 own greeneries ≈ 5 VP · price: −4-6 VP (city gen 3, first greenery gen 10, plants idle six gens; a rival made 14 tile VP on the same board) · (4p WGT gens 3-10, the player · ×1 seats)
- A city machine is a full VP plan: Immigrant City early + 5 cities + 5 greeneries → 9 adjacency VP + Mayor + Landlord · price: +23 VP from one plan · (4p WGT gens 3-10, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Put a VP-per-adjacent-city card beside a rival's city: you score, they lose the greenery slot · price: +2 VP and the adjacency blocked · (4p WGT gen 4, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Last-gen production counts for award metrics — price Zeppelins/Banker-class cards by the award, not by income · price: 12 MC → 6 VP (award flip + 1 VP) · (4p WGT gen 10, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Last-generation SP city beside three greeneries (25 MC → 3 VP) beats a second greenery (23 → 2); then a greenery beside that city is 3 VP more · price: +6 VP from 48 MC in the final generation · (3p gen 14, the player · ×1 seats)
- A forced-isolated city goes on the hex whose neighbours ALL carry plant bonuses — it becomes a second greenery cluster, not a dead tile · price: 3 adjacent greeneries; 17 city VP of an 88-point game · (3p gens 7-13, the player · ×1 seats)

## Plants / removal
- Never sit at 7+ plants across a rival's turn at an asteroid table: stay ≤5, or reach 8 and convert the SAME turn — surviving a hoard is luck, not a rule · price: 10 held twice → −14 plants ≈ −6 VP; 7 plants ate an asteroid pair and the greenery never landed; 9 and 11 survived only because both asteroids hit someone else (~4 VP exposed); eleven plants lost early to two asteroid strikes, then every later threshold converted the same turn → 11 greenery VP · (4p WGT gens 5-9; 3p gens 3-9, the player · ×4 seats)
- Aim every removal rider at the biggest plant holder — by count, never by grudge — and book denied greeneries as VP · price: 3 asteroids = 9 TR + ~3 denied greeneries ≈ 6-8 VP, the winning margin; temp + ocean + a rival's last 3 plants in one card · (4p WGT gens 5/6/9 · ×2 seats)
- Arctic Algae is a must-keep at 3p+ WHEN played early with at least four oceans still to come and its temperature window safe: every ocean pays, WGT oceans included — do not keep it late or with the oceans nearly done · price: ~12 free plants from gen 4; ~14 plants when played with seven oceans remaining · (4p WGT gen 4, a rival; 3p gen 5, the player · ×2 seats)
- A card that PUSHES you over 8 plants is only playable in a turn where the conversion also fits — sequenced second it spends the action that would have banked the greenery · price: 5→9 plants with no action left; an asteroid took 8 that round ≈ −19 MC of greenery · (4p gen 3, the player · ×1 seats)
- Once removal has landed at the table, stop accumulating plants and buy greeneries with MC — the MC route cannot be attacked · price: 11 plants lost across two attacks; the SP line that replaced it placed 10 greeneries untouched · (4p gens 3-7, the player · ×1 seats)

## TR engines
- Energy goes to heat, then TR — never sold 1:1 for MC · price: 3 Power Plant SPs + ~30 energy cashed while heat sat at 3: −3-4 TR, enough to win · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Energy production needs a TR outlet (converter, Magnetizer-class, heat) and is never bought after gen 5 for a heat step — the same MC is asteroids · price: 6 energy prod fed only sinks → lowest TR at the table; 38 MC of mirrors → 2 TR vs 2-3 asteroids · (4p WGT gens 5-9 · ×2 seats)
- A gen-1 repeatable-TR pair is the best TR rate in the game · price: Nuclear Power + Equatorial Magnetizer = 8 TR for 21 MC over 8 gens · (4p WGT gen 1, the player · ×1 seats)
- An engine with one stealable input needs its second input bought BEFORE the attack — but ABANDONING it is not the alternative to protecting it: drop a repeatable TR source only once you have NAMED its replacement, and never spend its input on other cards afterwards · price: an energy steal in gen 2 cut ~3 TR/gen to 1 and the backup came gen 8; a second reading dropped the only repeatable TR card in hand after ONE steal with nine gens left, then spent its 4-energy input on two other plays — TR ended 27 v 58 · (4p WGT gens 2-8 + 2p CE gens 4-12, the player · ×2 seats+models)
- Convert 8 heat the turn you reach it unless holding for a Thermalist LOCK · price: 9 heat parked three gens = −2-3 TR of tempo · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Buy a repeatable TR action only in the generation you can actually RUN it — then use it EVERY generation the parameter lasts; late buys still pay · price: an ocean action idle with money in hand = −2 TR; bought gen 4, first used gen 11 ≈ 4-6 TR forgone; bought at 18 in gen 7 it still made +4 TR and 4 bonuses · (4p WGT + 3p + 2p CE gens 3-12 · ×4 seats+models)
- Gen-1 TR is at-rate when it comes with placement bonuses · price: 23 MC → 2 oceans, 2 TR, 4 resources — the best opening at the table · (4p WGT gen 1, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Terraform WITH the engine from gen 5: +2 TR/gen, plants/heat held only until the next conversion · price: TR 27 vs 45; and TR 27 v 58 for a cash engine that led MC production 15 to 2 yet finished POORER on income (27+15 against 58+2), raising its first parameter only in gen 10 of 12 · (4p WGT + 2p CE gens 5-12, the player · ×2 seats+models)
- Physics Complex is an elite VP engine when energy production is exactly 6 and doubled against steals: 2 VP per gen from gen 6 · price: 18 VP, best card on the table; one activation lost to a steal ≈ 2 VP · (3p gens 5-14, the player · ×1 seats)
- Oxygen steps are a DEPLETING TR source: once the track maxes, greeneries drop to pure tile VP — take greenery TR early, and when one step remains buy it AHEAD of any cheaper play · price: two pre-max greeneries carried +2 TR the later four did not; a 23 MC standard-project greenery bought for the LAST oxygen step ahead of a first-player rival holding a ready greenery scored 3 VP where waiting scored 2 · (3p gens 9-13 + 2p CE gen 12, the player · ×2 seats+models)
- When a rival's engine terraforms for free, let it — it opens your requirement-locked windows at zero cost · price: 16 rival heat prod moved temperature 20 steps and opened two held cards · (3p gens 8-12, the player · ×1 seats)

## Card VP
- From gen 6, buy printed VP at ≤5 MC/VP over TR at 10-14 MC/TR — and refuse 1-VP cards at 8-19 MC while a milestone (8 → 5 VP) or asteroid is on the menu · price: passed VP cards = card VP 7 vs 22/17 (−4-6 VP); ~9 overpriced 1-VP cards ≈ 10 MC/VP · (4p WGT, both sides · ×2 seats)
- A Credicor Jovian pile is a 65-point line by itself; it needs one more cheap TR source, not more VP · price: 16 VP + 4 TR from five cards; lost by 2 · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Negative-VP cards are fine early and wrong after gen 8 unless net ≥ +2 VP — and QUOTE the printed VP in the valuation line of every card BEFORE buying it: an unread printed −1 silently cancels the +1 TR you paid for · price: 3 negative VP = the whole gap to the winner; a gen-7 buy priced as "1 TR + 2 cards + 4 MC denial for 8 MC" was net ≈ 0 VP and fed a −4 negative-VP total, worst at the table, in a match lost by 1 · (4p WGT gens 7/10, a rival and the player · ×2 seats)
- Accumulators can be fed by riders (Imported Hydrogen, Local Heat Trapping) — no own animal source needed · price: Predators 4 VP · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- A card-draw engine plus 30+ cards played is the strongest VP proxy at a hidden-VP table — aim removal at the card-rich seat, not the tile-rich one · price: a draw-engine rival scored 28 card VP where the tracker credited 15; removal misdirected ≈ 4-6 VP · (3p gens 11-12, the player · ×1 seats)
- Adaptation Technology is a must-keep with a requirement-heavy hand: ±2 opened five cards ≈ 6 VP + 4 energy prod for 12 MC · price: ≈ 2 MC/VP of enablement · (3p gens 3-9, the player · ×1 seats)
- Buying a card purely for its TAG is legitimate — say so in the plan and never budget for its action · price: a 6 MC building tag completed an 8-tag milestone (+5 VP); its 10 MC action was correctly never used · (3p gen 7, the player · ×1 seats)
- Price a per-generation lottery card against the ACTION SLOT, not just its MC · price: a 1 MC reveal action used eleven times for 0 VP — 14 MC and 11 slots against a 3 VP ceiling · (3p gens 1-13, the player · ×1 seats)

## Economy timing
- Drop a keep the moment its engine dies; sell, don't carry · price: three dead keeps = −3 MC each + hand clog · (4p WGT, the player · ×1 seats)
- Play a production card the generation it unlocks; a good card unplayed is the most expensive card in hand · price: a plant-prod card bought gen 4, playable gen 6, never played ≈ 3-4 VP · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- A corp's tag discount is an MC-production line: stack the tag · price: Earth tags took MC prod 1 → 6 → 11 · (4p WGT gens 4-7, the player · ×1 seats)
- Credicor: the turn-1 20+ MC production card funds the game; keep buying 20+ cards · price: +5 MC prod gen 1; 8+ rebates ≈ 32 MC · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Never pass or sell with playable TR/VP in hand · price: a 2-TR event and two VP cards sold or unplayed · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Late one-shot cash beats production: it pays for THIS turn's VP · price: +10 MC loan → the VP card the same turn · (4p WGT gen 8, the player · ×1 seats)
- Ban a self-inflicted −MC-prod start without an immediate refill · price: gen-3 city at −1 MC prod needed a gen-10 rescue · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Cash without cards is a real bottleneck: 40 MC + idle steel against a locked two-card hand means the next buy is DRAW, not production · price: three draw engines in one generation turned two cards into eight and produced the milestone-completer · (3p gens 6-7, the player · ×1 seats)
- A corp tag discount stacks with an in-play discount of the same tag · price: two −3s + a −1 made an 8 MC card cost 1 for +5 MC production · (3p gen 7, the player · ×1 seats)
- Unspent metal at game end is the largest leak a steel corp has — and price a metal-hoarding rival by what they SPEND, not what they hold · price: 63 idle steel ≈ 5 SP greeneries ≈ 10-15 VP unrealised · (3p gen 14, a rival · ×1 seats)

- Standard Technology is priced by the standard projects you will COMMIT to: three refunds break even, and a cash-rich tile plan or a 7-plant greenery corp turns it into an elite early keep · price: at least seven 3 MC refunds ≈ 21 MC returned on ~9 MC of keep + play · (3p gens 3-11, the player · ×1 seats)
- Two consecutive zero-buy research phases is a hard trigger to re-derive the WHOLE plan, never a per-phase judgement: consecutive declines signal a position that can no longer use cards (missing production base) while the window to buy one is closing, and a standing buy-0 order must never suppress LOOKING at the offer · price: one card bought in eight generations, 10 of 24 offered cards dead to missing energy/steel production, the two final offers never even viewed; ~2-4 VP sat in the offers themselves but the position that deadened them lost by 8 · (5p WGT, real opponents, gens 2-9, the player · ×1 humans)

## Tempo & process
- Last generation: asteroids, awards, milestones, printed VP, conversions ONLY — no draw engines, no production · price: ≈ 20-25 MC of dead late buys = 2-3 TR · (4p WGT gens 9-10, a rival · ×1 seats)
- After gen 6 money buys TR (12-14 MC) or a milestone (8 MC), not production · price: two more TR = first place · (4p WGT, a rival · ×1 seats)
- Check the milestone/award board before every turn; race table first, cards second · price: a −10 VP swing and an unpriced tie · (4p WGT, both sides · ×1 seats)
- Expect the leader to be attacked ~3× per game; price each attack at one engine use · price: three attacks all landed on the leader · (4p WGT gens 2/5, a rival attacking the player · ×1 seats)
- WGT: 9 free steps earn nobody TR and cut the game a generation short — run the clock one gen shorter · price: production cutoff at gen 6 · (4p WGT, all seats · ×1 seats)
- Never spend a WGT step on a track whose FINAL step is in your own plan: WGT grants nobody TR, so burning that track deletes the +1 TR your own later conversion would have earned — pick the track nobody at the table will cap, and spend the step where its FINAL increment denies a rival conversion · price: 1 TR — the last oxygen step went to the player's own greenery two generations after the advised WGT oxygen step would have deleted it; three steps spent ocean / temperature / temperature-final and NONE on oxygen while greeneries were the player's own TR track, the last of them taking the final temperature step specifically to kill the heat→temperature conversion two rivals sat one step from · (5p WGT, real opponents, gens 4-8; 4p WGT gens 1/5/9, the player · ×2 humans+seats)
- Re-run the saturation check against LIVE state at the moment of the pass — your OWN actions can revive a requirement-gated card mid-turn · price: an SP took oceans to 9 and un-blocked a 1-VP card after the pass was sent · (4p gen 12, the player · ×1 seats)
- Read affordability off the menu, never off mental arithmetic — a discount silently changes the price · price: 0 this time; the stated blocker was false · (4p gen 5, the player · ×1 seats)
- Keep turn time near the table's pace; decide answer-first · price: a seat at 56 min vs 26-42 made the table wait a third of the game · (4p WGT, the player · ×1 seats)

## Player-count calibration
- At 2p there is NO second-place award VP — every award is a clean 5-0 and only three slots exist, so funding the three metrics your ARCHETYPE owns both scores 15 and denies the rival everything; read the value out of the engine (score.py AWARD_VP), never from the 4-5p intuition that second place is accidental value · price: 42 MC → 15 VP to me and 0 to a rival who led Landlord 15 tiles to 2 and Thermalist 11 heat to 3 · (2p CE gens 5-8, the player · ×1 models)
- One-off VP purchases (milestones, awards, printed VP, standard-project tiles) have a CEILING and an engine does not: once the purchasable bucket is bought, a seat with no repeatable parameter source loses ground at a fixed rate every remaining generation · price: level at −4 VP in gen 7 when a milestone and two awards landed, then −22 VP over the last five generations at ~4.5/gen; a tile+milestone seat that bought ONE card in eight generations and drew level on tiles led gens 4-7, then lost the final three generations −30 to −11 and the game by 8 · (2p CE gens 7-12, both sides; 5p WGT, real opponents, gens 4-9, the player · ×2 models+humans)
- A 3p base+CE game runs 13-14 generations, not 10-11 — production bought gens 5-6 pays 8 gens, and even a gen-10 production card paid four times · price: the short-clock prior would have skipped ≈ 6-8 VP of engine · (3p gens 1-14, the player · ×1 seats)
- At 3p the milestone and award buckets decide games: three slots for three seats — pick your first target by gen 3, claim-first, and SWEEP all three when cash allows; the bucket is 15 VP no rival can claw back, and awards + tile geometry beat card-VP piles · price: the highest-TR seat (45, nine clear) finished LAST claiming none; a cluster + two milestones + 32 cards beat a 40-TR engine by 16; 12 award VP + 23 greenery/city VP with only 2 card VP beat rivals holding 17 and 23 card VP · (3p, three games, winner and player sides · ×3 seats)
