# Strategy KB 1 — Valuation & Economy (Corporate Era, 3–5 players)

The advisor reads this before evaluating any card or action. Values are
community-derived heuristics (rooted in BGG's "A Quantified Guide to TM
Strategy" tradition), tuned for multiplayer Corporate Era. They are priors,
not laws — adjust for game length and board state.

## Resource values in MC (rules of thumb)
- 1 steel ≈ 2 MC; 1 titanium ≈ 3 MC (their build values; slightly less if
  you rarely play matching tags)
- 1 plant ≈ 2 MC (8 plants → greenery worth ~14+: 1 TR + 1 VP + adjacency)
- 1 heat ≈ 1 MC (8 heat → temperature = 1 TR)
- 1 card in hand ≈ 2 MC raw; ≈ 3+ MC if your economy can play what you draw
- 1 energy ≈ 1 MC as heat-to-be; more if cards consume it
- 1 VP ≈ 5 MC late, cheaper if bought early via efficient cards
- 1 TR ≈ 1 VP + ~1 MC income per remaining generation. TR is the only thing
  that pays income AND score at once, so price a TR step against MC
  PRODUCTION, not only against VP (observed at 2p: MC production 15 to 2, yet
  final income 42/gen against 60/gen, because TR was 27 against 58 — 06 TR
  engines). This is a VALUATION correction, NOT a mandate to race TR: two
  games in 06's ledger were won while LOSING TR by 7 and by 9, on milestones,
  awards and tile adjacency. The rule is that every engine needs a named
  scoring exit — TR is one of three (tiles, card VP, meta are the others),
  and the loss case is having none. Early TR compounds:
  TR in gen 3 with 9 gens left ≈ 5 (VP) + 9 (income) worth ~ 14 MC of value.

## Production values (multiply by remaining generations)
- +1 MC prod ≈ (gens remaining) MC. Worth ~9 MC in gen 3, ~3 MC in gen 9.
- +1 steel prod ≈ 1.7× MC prod value if you play building tags regularly.
- +1 plant prod ≈ 2× MC prod value (feeds greeneries → TR+VP).
- +1 heat prod ≈ 1× MC prod value (pure TR fuel).
- +1 energy prod ≈ context: 1× as future heat; more with consumers/converters.
- THE CUTOFF: production cards stop paying around 3–4 generations before game
  end. From then on, buy points and TR directly.

## Card economics
- Playing a card costs its printed cost + the 3 MC you paid to keep it.
  A kept-but-never-played card is −3 MC; be selective at research phase.
- Payback test: production card value ≈ prod/gen × gens remaining − total
  cost. Positive by a comfortable margin or skip.
- Card draw engines are top-tier in 2p, still strong in 5p; never
  undervalue "look at / take cards" effects.
- Discounts (per-card cost reduction) scale with cards played — excellent
  early, dead late.

## Game-length awareness (drives everything)
- 5 players: expect ~8–10 generations; parameters fill FAST.
- Track: oceans left, temp steps left, O2 steps left. When any two are near
  done, shift from engine to points NOW.
- Requirement windows cut both ways: "max X" cards (O2 ≤ 5%, temp ≤ −14)
  die as the board progresses — play or discard them early; "min X" cards
  unlock — time purchases so they don't rot in hand.
- Free land is the FOURTH clock (observed in a 5-player game that ran 17
  generations: 97 unconverted plants and 10 plant production on a board
  with 0 free land). The brief prints `Free land: N`;
  at ≤ 6 hexes plant production and tile placers stop paying — price them
  at zero and pivot to printed-VP cards and resource accumulators.

## Action economy
- Standard projects are overpriced on purpose (Aquifer 18, City 25) —
  they're a cash sink for when cards are better spent; use late or when
  racing a milestone/parameter.
- Heat→temp and plants→greenery conversions are free actions economically:
  always take them when available unless saving for a strategic burst.
- Turn order: 2 actions per turn; sequence so opponents can't react
  (e.g. claim contested milestone before revealing your production jump).

## Validation vs BGG 1847708 "Quantified Guide"
Source-of-truth prices when evaluating a card's efficiency — these are
the guide's card-derived costs (incl. 3 MC keep): energy prod 7, heat
prod 6, plant prod 10, steel prod 8, titanium prod 10, MC income 5,
temp step 10, ocean 14, TR 10, VP 5, science tag ~4–5. SPs cost ~4 MC
above card rate. Corrections/precisions to the rules of thumb above:
- Windfall steel/titanium/plants/cards ≈ 2 MC each as OPPORTUNITY value
  (board bonuses make them interchangeable); steel ≈ 2 / titanium ≈ 3 MC
  remain their BUILD values when actually spent on matching tags.
- "1 MC prod ≈ gens remaining" is the dynamic view above; the guide prices
  income flat at 5 MC per +1 MC/gen (≈10-gen game). Use the flat 5 for
  quick card-rate checks, the dynamic view near the production cutoff.
- "1 TR ≈ 14 MC in gen 3" refines the guide's flat TR = 10 MC; both agree
  TR bought EARLY beats late.
- Card draw: useless card = 1 MC (sell), useful ≈ 3 MC while card-rich,
  ≈ 7 MC when card-poor (rich engine, empty hand) — draw engines peak
  early or in cash-flush late games.
